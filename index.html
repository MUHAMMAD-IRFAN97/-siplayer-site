SI Player Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

SI Player ("the app") is an offline video and music player for Android. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

SI Player does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app does not require an account or sign-in.

How the app works

The app works fully offline. It plays video and audio files that are already stored on your device. It does not stream content from the internet and does not upload your files anywhere.

Permissions

The app asks for permission to access video and audio files on your device. This is used only to show and play your media inside the app. Your files stay on your device.

Third parties

SI Player does not share data with third parties and does not include advertising or analytics services.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
